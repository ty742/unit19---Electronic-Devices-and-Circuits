🎯 Assessment Journey & Milestones

Key delivery phases and official Pearson summative assignment deadlines.

AIM A (7 Weeks)
Deadline 23 Oct 2026

Analogue Devices & Commercial Circuits

Investigate safe electronic practices, diodes (signal, rectifier, Zener), BJT/FET transistors, and operational amplifier circuits (comparators, filters, inverting/non-inverting amps).

Criteria: A.P1, A.P2, A.P3, A.M1, A.M2, A.M3, A.D1
AIM B (9 Weeks)
Deadline 15 Jan 2027

Digital Logic Devices & Circuits

Design, simulate and construct combinational logic (Boolean algebra, Karnaugh maps, De Morgan's) and sequential circuits (R-S, D-type, JK flip-flops, counters, shift registers).

Criteria: B.P4, B.P5, B.P6, B.M4, B.M5, B.M6, B.D2
AIM C (2 Weeks)
Deadline 29 Jan 2027

Engineering Review & Reflection

Evaluate personal engineering performance, compare theoretical vs simulated vs measured values, and review professional laboratory behaviours and lessons learned.

Criteria: C.P7, C.P8, C.M7, C.D3

📚 Weekly Lab Modules

Progressive 160-minute workplace-modeled engineering investigations.

Learning Aim A • Weeks 1–5

Analogue Electronics

Safe electronic working practices, diode rectification & Zener regulation, transistor switching & amplification, operational amplifiers, and prototype verification.

Week 1 • Live 7 Sep 2026

Unit 19 Launch: “Can You Make It Work?”

Role: Junior Electronics Technician

Build baseline LED circuit, confirm operation, then diagnose a production test failure using the Observe → Predict → Test → Locate → Fix → Verify troubleshooting cycle.

PoE: A01 Evidence Launch Module →
Week 2 • Live 14 Sep 2026

Diode Investigation: “Protect & Control Power”

Role: Junior Power Electronics Engineer

Explore forward/reverse bias, PN-junction characteristics, half/full-wave rectification, capacitor smoothing, and Zener voltage regulation under real load conditions.

PoE: A02 Evidence Launch Module →
Week 3 • Live 21 Sep 2026

Transistor Control: “Switching & Amplification”

Role: Junior Control Engineer

Investigate BJT/FET devices, cut-off, active, and saturation states. Build a transistor load switch and measure small-signal common-emitter gain and AC frequency sweep.

PoE: A03 Evidence Launch Module →
Week 4 • Live 28 Sep 2026

Op-Amp Conditioning: “Condition Sensor Signals”

Role: Junior Instrumentation Engineer

Simulate open-loop comparator and closed-loop inverting/non-inverting amplifier stages in Multisim. Compare theoretical gain calculations with virtual oscilloscope waveforms and phase inversion.

Multisim Simulation Lab Launch Module →
Week 5 • Live 5 Oct 2026

Prototype Verification: “Prove the Design Works”

Role: Prototype / Verification Test Engineer

Audit and verify analogue diode rectifier and Zener voltage regulator circuits against assignment requirements. Measure regulated DC voltage, quantify ripple across defined loads (22 kΩ, 10 kΩ, 4.7 kΩ), and perform multi-domain error analysis.

Hardware & Simulation Verification Lab Launch Module →
Learning Aim B • Starting 2 Nov 2026

Digital Electronics

Combinational logic gates, truth tables, Boolean algebra, Karnaugh maps, sequential flip-flop circuits, counters, shift registers, and digital control system integration.

Week 1 • Live 2 Nov 2026

Digital Launch: “Can You Build the Decision?”

Role: Junior Control & Digital Systems Engineer

Investigate AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR and XOR logic gates, simulate truth tables in Multisim, attend the Careers & Employability talk, and assemble a 5V SPDT LED stripboard circuit.

PoE: B01 Evidence Launch Module →
Week 2 • Live 9 Nov 2026

Combinational Logic: “Design the Control System”

Role: Junior Control Systems Engineer

Translate industrial specifications into truth tables, derive Boolean expressions, construct combinational logic with 74LS ICs, simulate in Multisim, and verify on a physical breadboard.

Practical Investigation Launch Module →
Week 3 16 Nov 2026

Boolean Algebra: “Can You Make It Simpler?”

Role: Production Optimisation Engineer

Apply Boolean laws and De Morgan's theorem to simplify an inefficient logic circuit, reducing IC gate count while verifying identical functional behaviour.

PoE: B03 Evidence Available Week 3
Week 4 23 Nov 2026

Karnaugh Maps: “Find the Shortest Route”

Role: Digital Design Engineer

Construct and group Karnaugh maps (K-maps) from control truth tables to derive minimal Boolean expressions and build the lowest gate-count implementation.

PoE: B04 Evidence Available Week 4
Week 5 30 Nov 2026

Sequential Logic: “Give the System a Memory”

Role: Automation Systems Engineer

Distinguish combinational and sequential logic, and investigate RS, D-type, and JK flip-flops to retain digital state information across clock pulses.

PoE: B05 Evidence Available Week 5
Week 6 7 Dec 2026

Counters: “Make It Count”

Role: Production Line Test Engineer

Explain clock pulses and binary counting, construct a three-stage counter, predict output state sequences, and diagnose faults in a sequential circuit.

PoE: B06 Evidence Available Week 6
Week 7 14 Dec 2026

Shift Registers: “Move the Data”

Role: Embedded Data Engineer

Investigate serial and parallel data transfer using shift registers, predicting and verifying stored bit patterns after successive clock pulses.

PoE: B07 Evidence Available Week 7
Weeks 8–9 4–11 Jan 2027

Digital Prototype Integration & Design Review

Role: Verification & Systems Engineer

Combine combinational and sequential building blocks into a complete digital controller prototype and evaluate test evidence for Learning Aim B sign-off.

PoE: B08 & B09 Available Week 8
