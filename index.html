🎯 Assessment Journey & Milestones

Key delivery phases and official Pearson summative assignment deadlines.

AIM A (7 Weeks)
Deadline 23 Oct 2026

Analogue Devices & Commercial Circuits

Investigate safe electronic practices, diodes (signal, rectifier, Zener), BJT/FET transistors, and operational amplifier circuits (comparators, filters, inverting/non-inverting amps).

Criteria: A.P1, A.P2, A.P3, A.M1, A.M2, A.M3, A.D1
AIM B (9 Weeks)
Deadline 15 Jan 2027

Digital Logic Devices & Circuits

Design, simulate and construct combinational logic (Boolean algebra, Karnaugh maps, De Morgan's) and sequential circuits (R-S, D-type, JK flip-flops, counters, shift registers).

Criteria: B.P4, B.P5, B.P6, B.M4, B.M5, B.M6, B.D2
AIM C (2 Weeks)
Deadline 29 Jan 2027

Engineering Review & Reflection

Evaluate personal engineering performance, compare theoretical vs simulated vs measured values, and review professional laboratory behaviours and lessons learned.

Criteria: C.P7, C.P8, C.M7, C.D3

📚 Learning Aim A: Weekly Lab Modules

Progressive 160-minute workplace-modeled engineering investigations.

Week 1 • Live 7 Sep 2026

Unit 19 Launch: “Can You Make It Work?”

Role: Junior Electronics Technician

Build baseline LED circuit, confirm operation, then diagnose a production test failure using the Observe → Predict → Test → Locate → Fix → Verify troubleshooting cycle.

PoE: A01 Evidence Launch Module →
Week 2 • Live 14 Sep 2026

Diode Investigation: “Protect & Control Power”

Role: Junior Power Electronics Engineer

Explore forward/reverse bias, PN-junction characteristics, half/full-wave rectification, capacitor smoothing, and Zener voltage regulation under real load conditions.

PoE: A02 Evidence Launch Module →
Week 3 • Live 21 Sep 2026

Transistor Control: “Switching & Amplification”

Role: Junior Control Engineer

Investigate BJT/FET devices, cut-off, active, and saturation states. Build a transistor load switch and measure small-signal common-emitter gain and AC frequency sweep.

PoE: A03 Evidence Launch Module →
Week 4 • Live 28 Sep 2026

Op-Amp Conditioning: “Condition Sensor Signals”

Role: Junior Instrumentation Engineer

Simulate open-loop comparator and closed-loop inverting/non-inverting amplifier stages in Multisim. Compare theoretical gain calculations with virtual oscilloscope waveforms and phase inversion.

Multisim Simulation Lab Launch Module →
Week 5 • Live 5 Oct 2026

Prototype Verification: “Prove the Design Works”

Role: Prototype / Verification Test Engineer

Audit and verify analogue diode rectifier and Zener voltage regulator circuits against assignment requirements. Measure regulated DC voltage, quantify ripple across defined loads (22 kΩ, 10 kΩ, 4.7 kΩ), and perform multi-domain error analysis.

Hardware & Simulation Verification Lab Launch Module →
Weeks 6–7 12–19 Oct 2026

Verification Analysis & Design Review

Role: Junior Electronics Engineer

Synthesise simulation, test data, and tolerance variations to deliver the technical evaluation report for Learning Aim A.

PoE: A06 & A07 Available Week 6
