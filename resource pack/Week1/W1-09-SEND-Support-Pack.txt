2. BEFORE YOU POWER
============================================================

STOP.

Ask yourself:

1. Is the power supply OFF?

YES / NO

2. Is the supply set to the correct voltage?

YES / NO

3. Is the polarity correct?

YES / NO

4. Are the components in the correct positions?

YES / NO

5. Are the components facing the correct way?

YES / NO

6. Are the wires connected correctly?

YES / NO

7. Are there any obvious shorts?

YES / NO

8. Do I know what the circuit should do?

YES / NO

9. Do I know what I am going to measure?

YES / NO

If any answer is NO:

STOP AND ASK FOR HELP.

============================================================

- Correct value

- Other polarity-sensitive components

9. TROUBLESHOOTING CHECKLIST
============================================================

Work through the list one item at a time.

[ ] Check component values.

[ ] Check wiring.

engineering process.

12. TECHNICAL VOCABULARY BANK
============================================================

SCHEMATIC

A diagram showing how components are electrically connected.

POLARITY

The required positive/negative orientation of a component or
connection.

CONTINUITY

An unbroken electrical connection.

FAULT

A condition causing a circuit to behave incorrectly.

MEASUREMENT

A value obtained using a measuring instrument.

PREDICTION

A reasoned expectation of what should happen.

VERIFICATION

Evidence that confirms whether something works as intended.

ISOLATION

Disconnecting a source of energy so that equipment can be worked
on safely.

HAZARD

Something that has the potential to cause harm.

CONTROL MEASURE

An action used to reduce or control a hazard.
