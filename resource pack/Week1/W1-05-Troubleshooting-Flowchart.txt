============================================================
YOUR ENGINEERING APPROACH
============================================================

When an electronic circuit does not work, your first instinct
might be to change a component.

DO NOT.

Use a systematic process.

        OBSERVE
           ↓
       PREDICT
           ↓
          TEST
           ↓
         LOCATE
           ↓
           FIX
           ↓
         VERIFY
           ↓
        DOCUMENT

Your job is not simply to make the circuit work.

Your job is to understand WHY it did not work and prove that
your repair has solved the problem.

============================================================

Record the symptom before changing anything.

Examples:

- LED does not illuminate.
- LED is too bright.
- LED is too dim.
- Output voltage is incorrect.
- Circuit behaves intermittently.
- Component becomes unusually warm.
- No output is present.

COMMON TROUBLESHOOTING CHECKS
============================================================

Depending on the circuit, consider:

[ ] Supply voltage

[ ] Supply polarity

[ ] Ground/0 V connection

[ ] Component orientation

[ ] Component value

[ ] Component type

[ ] Breadboard row/connection

[ ] Jumper wire

[ ] Switch position

[ ] Continuity

[ ] Short circuit

[ ] Open circuit

[ ] Incorrect meter setting

[ ] Incorrect test point

[ ] Damaged component

IMPORTANT:

Do not carry out a test simply because it is available.

Choose a test because it answers a question.

============================================================
TEAM CHALLENGE
============================================================

One learner acts as the:

INVESTIGATION ENGINEER

The other acts as:

TEST ENGINEER

The Investigation Engineer explains the hypothesis.

The Test Engineer challenges it:

“What evidence do you have?”

“What would prove that?”

“What result would you expect?”

Swap roles.

============================================================
FINAL ENGINEERING RULE
============================================================

NEVER SAY:

“I think this component is faulty.”

WITHOUT ALSO BEING ABLE TO SAY:

“Here is the evidence that makes me think it is faulty.”

The difference between guessing and engineering is:

EVIDENCE.
