PURPOSE
============================================================

This checklist supports formative observation during the Week 1
practical activity.

The purpose is to identify:

- Existing practical competence
- Safety awareness
- Troubleshooting approach
- Measurement skills
- Communication/teamwork
- Areas requiring support
- Appropriate stretch opportunities

This is primarily a FORMATIVE observation tool.

It should help inform future teaching and retrieval activities.

8. TROUBLESHOOTING PROCESS
============================================================

Observe:

[ ] Learner identifies expected behaviour.

[ ] Learner identifies actual symptom.

Predict:

[ ] Learner proposes a plausible cause.

[ ] Learner provides a reason for the prediction.

Test:

[ ] Learner selects an appropriate test.

[ ] Learner explains why the test is useful.

Locate:

[ ] Learner interprets the result.

[ ] Learner rules out possible causes.

Fix:

[ ] Learner identifies an appropriate corrective action.

Verify:

[ ] Learner retests the repaired circuit.

[ ] Learner proves that the repair worked.

Overall troubleshooting rating:

3 / 2 / 1 / 0

9. EVIDENCE-BASED REASONING
============================================================

Look for statements such as:

"The measurement suggests..."

"This result rules out..."

"I expected...but measured..."

"This could indicate..."

"I would test..."

"I know the repair worked because..."

Rate learner:

3 – Consistently evidence-based

2 – Usually evidence-based with prompting

1 – Frequently guesses

0 – Evidence-based reasoning not observed
