SCENARIO 1 – SMOKE FROM THE CIRCUIT
============================================================

You are testing a prototype circuit.

Suddenly, you notice a small amount of smoke coming from the
breadboard.

You are not sure what has caused it.

What should you do?

Below are possible actions.

A. Continue measuring the circuit.

B. Disconnect/isolate the electrical supply if it is safe and
   you are authorised to do so.

C. Touch the circuit to find out which component is hot.

D. Inform the tutor/responsible person.

E. Restart the circuit to see if the problem happens again.

F. Follow the laboratory emergency procedure.

G. Raise the alarm if required.

H. Leave the area/evacuate if instructed.

I. Carry on working because the smoke has stopped.

J. Record/report the incident through the correct procedure.

============================================================

Complete the following statements.

YES / NO

I have checked polarity:
YES / NO

YES / NO

YES / NO

If any answer is NO:

STOP.

Do not power the circuit.

Ask your tutor for assistance.

============================================================
THE ENGINEERING RULE
============================================================

Before applying power:

STOP
→ THINK
→ CHECK
→ VERIFY
→ POWER

Do not reverse this sequence.
