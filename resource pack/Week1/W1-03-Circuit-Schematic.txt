YOUR ROLE
============================================================

You are working as a junior electronics technician.

Your job is to interpret the circuit schematic before inspecting
and testing the physical prototype.

Remember:

A schematic tells another engineer how the circuit is intended
to operate.

Do not begin by guessing the fault.

Understand the circuit first.

============================================================
1. CIRCUIT PURPOSE
============================================================

The circuit is a simple low-voltage indicator circuit.

When the switch is closed:

- Current should flow through the resistor.
- The LED should illuminate.
- The resistor should limit the current through the LED.
- The circuit should operate safely from the specified supply.

Your task is to determine why the prototype does not behave as
expected.
